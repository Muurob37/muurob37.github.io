Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account identifiers: your TikTok open_id / username and connected page names, used only to show which account is linked and to publish to it.
  • Content metadata: video files, captions, and schedules you create in the App.
  • OAuth tokens: access/refresh tokens issued by TikTok and Meta, stored encrypted at rest on our private server.

2. How We Use It

Only to (a) publish the content you instruct us to publish, and (b) display status in your dashboard. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • No posting to accounts you do not own and authorize.
  • No third-party data sharing or analytics resale.
  • No storage of your TikTok password (OAuth only).

4. Retention & Deletion

Tokens and content remain until you disconnect your account or request deletion. Revoking access in your TikTok settings immediately invalidates our ability to act on your behalf.

5. Security

Tokens are stored on an access-controlled server with file permissions restricted to the service account. Traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.
